Refund Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Our refund promise

  • One-off purchases: if the dataset you bought is faulty, not as described, or not delivered, we will refund the purchase.
  • Monthly plans: if a delivery fails because of an error on our side, we will refund that month.

How to ask for a refund

You can contact us at contact@dataforgeit.digital or through the contact form. Please include the email address used for your PayPal payment and a short description of the problem. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method through PayPal.

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Our goods and services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. For major failures with the service, you are entitled to cancel your service contract and to a refund for the unused portion, or to compensation for its reduced value. You are also entitled to choose a refund or replacement for major failures with goods. If a failure does not amount to a major failure, you are entitled to have problems with the service or goods rectified in a reasonable time and, if this is not done, to cancel and obtain a refund. This policy is in addition to those rights.

Change of mind

Because data can be copied once delivered, we do not normally offer refunds for change of mind after delivery. This does not affect your rights above.
