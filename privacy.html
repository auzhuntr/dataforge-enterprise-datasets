Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

DataForge IT is a business name used by James David Leake, a sole trader (ABN 16 938 211 061), based in New South Wales, Australia. This policy explains how we handle personal information. You can contact us at contact@dataforgeit.digital or through the contact form.

What we collect

  • Contact form: the form opens your own email app; we receive the name, email address and details you choose to send.
  • Purchases: PayPal sends us your name, email address and transaction details. Card and bank details are handled by PayPal and are not shared with us.
  • Website hosting: our host (GitHub Pages) processes standard technical information such as IP address and browser type to deliver the website securely.

How we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry, deliver what you bought, provide support, handle refunds, and meet our legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your personal information and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

Only service providers needed to run the business: PayPal (payments), GitHub (website hosting), and Porkbun and our email provider (email). These providers may store data outside Australia, including in the United States.

Our datasets

Our datasets are built to exclude personal information. Every dataset is reviewed before delivery to check that it contains no personal information.

Chat assistant

The chat assistant on our website answers from a fixed set of pre-written answers inside your browser. What you type into it is not sent to us or stored.

Keeping, accessing and correcting your information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above and for record-keeping required by law. You can ask to access or correct your information, or make a privacy complaint, using our contact form. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
